About HACKVERSE

Designed for serious builders and future innovators.

HACKVERSE 2026 is not only a coding event. It is a complete innovation challenge where students from different branches can convert ideas into real impact.

Event Objective

To encourage students to identify meaningful problems, build creative solutions, collaborate effectively and present their work with confidence in front of faculty, judges and inter-college participants.

01Promote practical innovation and technical confidence.
02Support both software and hardware-based project teams.
03Create a professional platform for student leadership.

Evaluation Parameters

Each project will be evaluated on clarity, originality, working model/prototype, feasibility, social or industrial impact and presentation quality.

✦Innovation and uniqueness of idea.
✦Technical execution and demo quality.
✦Real-world usefulness and scalability.

Event Flow

Structured for smooth execution.

Clear flow helps participants, coordinators, faculty and judges experience a professional event.

01

Registration

Teams submit member details, college name, contact and selected domain.

02

Problem Statement

Teams choose a given problem or propose a strong self-defined problem.

03

Build Phase

Teams build prototype, UI, circuit, model, dashboard, demo or pitch material.

04

Final Evaluation

Judges review innovation, execution, impact, feasibility and presentation.

Guidelines

Professional participation rules.

These rules can be edited later based on final college approval and organizing committee decisions.

Team Size

Recommended team size: 2 to 4 members. Final limit can be changed as per committee decision.

Original Work

Projects should be original. Reused work must be clearly explained with improvements.

Submission

Teams must submit idea title, domain, short abstract and demo/presentation material.

Judging

Judging will focus on innovation, execution, feasibility, impact and presentation clarity.

Discipline

Teams must follow venue rules, timing, faculty instructions and event coordination flow.

Result

Judges’ decision will be final. Winners will receive recognition, prizes and certificates.
